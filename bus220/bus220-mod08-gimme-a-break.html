Gimme a Break
A job negotiation game at Sole Ambition 👟, a sporting goods and sneaker store on Fordham Road
The Setup

You are the job candidate negotiating with Kwame, the manager. Four rounds: wage & hours, shift type, schedule notice, and breaks. Score as many points as you can (points show how much you like a deal, not just the money).

You get up to 5 exchanges per round. After each move you will see why Kwame reacted as he did, plus a coach tip.

Who are 😼Mango and 🧠Solver?

😼Mango is the store mascot. She always has an opinion. Whether she's actually helpful...

🧠Solver is a perfectly rational agent with no ego and no need for fairness, just math. You will see its best deal after each round.

BATNA: Your Walk-Away Option

Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement is what you get if there is no deal, such as another job offer. A deal worth more than your BATNA beats walking away. A deal worth less does not.

Example: Your BATNA is 95 pts. An 80-pt offer? Counter or walk. A 105-pt offer? It beats walking away.

Kwame has a hidden BATNA too. Find a deal that beats both. If you walk away, or the exchanges run out, that round ends and you both take your BATNA.
