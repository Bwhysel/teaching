Flair Furniture's big showroom sale puzzle

    The Flair Furniture story

    Flair Furniture makes tables and chairs. Each table needs 3 hours of carpentry and 2 hours of painting. Each chair needs 4 hours of carpentry and 1 hour of painting. This month the shop has 2,400 carpentry hours and 1,000 painting hours.

    Marketing wants no more than 450 chairs (the warehouse is full of them) and at least 100 tables (the showroom is nearly bare). Each table earns $7 profit and each chair earns $5. Which mix of tables and chairs earns the most?
