The Bond Dealer

Your first day on a NYC bond desk. 10 trades. Buy low, sell high.

Each round

  1. Read the card. It tells you about the bond and gives a yield range.
  2. Drag the slider to pick a price. Watch the yield change. Higher price = lower yield.
  3. Submit. Rounds 1–5 you buy (press BID). Rounds 6–10 you sell (press OFFER).

Don't get (too) greedy

Buying

Lower price = more profit. Too low and the seller walks away.

Selling

Higher price = more profit. Too high and the buyer walks away.
Scoring Each trade = $10M face. Prices are per $100 of face.
Deal closes (dealer spread, 1% of face) +$100,000 Each $1 better than the hidden fair price +$100,000 Each $1 worse than fair price −$100,000 No deal $0
Buy at 98.50, fair price 99: $100,000 + $50,000 = $150,000
Overpay by $2: $100,000 − $200,000 = −$100,000

Clues on the card

  • Safer bond (strong rating, high demand) → lower yield → higher price.
  • Riskier bond (weak rating, bad news, rates rising) → higher yield → lower price.
